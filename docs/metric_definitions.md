# Metric Definitions

## Revenue
`SUM(billed_revenue)`

## Labor Cost
`SUM(labor_cost)` from employee-level job labor.

## Material Cost
`SUM(actual_material_cost)`

## Gross Profit
`Revenue - Labor Cost - Material Cost`

## Gross Margin %
`Gross Profit / Revenue`

## Average Job Value
`Revenue / Jobs Completed`

## Revenue per Labor Hour
`Revenue / Actual Labor Hours`

## Labor Variance Hours
`Actual Labor Hours - Estimated Labor Hours`

## Labor Variance %
`(Actual Labor Hours - Estimated Labor Hours) / Estimated Labor Hours`

## Material Variance
`Actual Material Cost - Estimated Material Cost`

## Estimate Win Rate
`Won Estimates / Estimates Sent`

## Rework Rate
`Rework Jobs / Completed Jobs`

## Weather Delay Rate
`Weather-Delayed Jobs / Completed Jobs`

## Customer Rating
Average rating across completed jobs.
