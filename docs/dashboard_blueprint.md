# Dashboard Build Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Purpose: Tell the owner how the business is performing in under 30 seconds.

Top cards:
1. Revenue
2. Gross Profit
3. Gross Margin %
4. Jobs Completed
5. Avg Job Value
6. Active Customers

Charts:
- Monthly Revenue & Gross Profit
- Gross Margin % by Month
- Revenue by Service
- Revenue by City
- Recurring vs One-Time Revenue

Owner questions:
- Are we growing?
- Are margins improving?
- Which services and markets matter most?

## Page 2 — Job Profitability
Primary visual:
Detailed job profitability table with conditional formatting.

Key fields:
Job, Customer, Service, Crew, Revenue, Estimated Hours, Actual Hours,
Labor Cost, Material Cost, Gross Profit, Gross Margin %, Rework Flag.

Charts:
- Gross Margin % by Service
- Labor Variance % by Crew
- Estimated vs Actual Material Cost
- Bottom 15 Jobs by Gross Profit

Owner questions:
- Which jobs are losing money?
- Are we estimating labor correctly?
- Which services are underpriced?

## Page 3 — Crew Performance
Charts:
- Revenue per Labor Hour by Crew
- Labor Variance % by Crew
- Rework Rate by Crew
- Customer Rating by Crew
- Average Travel Minutes by Crew

Owner questions:
- Which crews are most efficient?
- Where are labor overruns happening?
- Is rework concentrated with one crew?

## Page 4 — Sales & Estimates
Charts:
- Estimate Win Rate by Service
- Estimate Win Rate by Acquisition Channel
- Quoted Revenue by Month
- Won vs Lost Estimate Value
- Average Estimate Size

Owner questions:
- Which services are easiest to sell?
- Which lead sources convert?
- Are larger jobs harder to close?

## Page 5 — Customers & Service Area
Charts:
- Revenue by City
- Customer Count by City
- Revenue by Customer Type
- Revenue by Acquisition Channel
- Top Customers
- Avg Travel Minutes by City

Owner questions:
- Where should we market?
- Which locations create route inefficiency?
- Are commercial customers worth the extra travel?
